# Top 12 Duo playoff qualification

The league conversion and saved playoff settings support Top 4/8/12/16. Top 12 is available only with at least 12 approved entries. A Duo entry is a two-player team; the qualifier count does not count individual players.

For Global Seeded or Protected Seed with one overall table, the strongest four seeds enter the quarter-finals directly. Seeds 5–12 play four play-ins: 8–9, 5–12, 7–10, 6–11. The quarter-finals pair 1–winner PI1, 4–winner PI2, 2–winner PI3, 3–winner PI4, followed by two semi-finals and one final. There are 11 played fixtures. Byes create no match, score or statistics event.

Double-Chance remains a separate, existing format: the top four seeds enter qualifying finals and the other eight follow the lower elimination path. Its 12-team graph has 13 fixtures; the four standard-knockout byes do not apply.

Saving settings preserves league fixtures, results and standings. Generate the official bracket only after all stage fixtures finish. Existing playoff fixtures lock settings changes. No production tournament is automatically converted, reconfigured or redrawn by deployment. No schema migration is required.

Coverage includes actual HTTP configuration of 14 Duo teams/28 players, preservation of 91 confirmed league matches, selection of the top 12 team IDs, four genuine play-ins/four seeded byes, connected advancement, no bye statistics, incomplete-stage and repeated-generation locks. Browser checks cover creation/editing, all three previews, refresh persistence and fewer-than-12 protection at 320/390/1280 widths.
